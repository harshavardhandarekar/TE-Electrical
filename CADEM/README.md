# 📐 CADEM – Computer aided design of Electric Machines

A complete collection of **CADEM study material and engineering drawing resources** for **Third Year Electrical Engineering (TE Electrical)** students as per the **SPPU 2024 Pattern**.

## ✨ Features

📚 CADEM study material  
📐 Engineering drawings  
📄 PDF drawing files  
💻 CAD drawing files  
⬇️ Direct PDF & CAD file downloads  
🗂️ Organized and easy-to-access resources  

## 📂 Contents

- 📄 **Engineering Drawing PDFs**
- 💻 **CAD Drawing Files**

## 🎯 Purpose

This folder is created to provide **TE Electrical Engineering students** with CADEM study material and engineering drawings in an organized manner.

Students can easily **view and download PDF and CAD drawing files directly**, without the need for ZIP files.

## 🛠️ For

**Third Year Electrical Engineering (TE Electrical)**  
**SPPU – 2024 Pattern**
